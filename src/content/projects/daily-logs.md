Daily Logs is an iOS life logging app built with SwiftUI. It is intentionally **small** in scope: the goal is to make recurring daily records light enough to keep using, rather than turning personal tracking into a chore.

The app supports manual sleep and wake logging, Apple Health sleep sync, meal records with optional photos, shower tracking, notes, analytics, localization, and Firebase-backed sync for signed-in users. It also includes optional AI-generated daily insights rating sleep quality, meal balance, and overall day quality based on the user's logs. Users cam easily track their habits and review their history, photos and notes in a clean, intuitive interface.

The app is currently under active development and has begun receiving internal testing through TestFlight. Please [contact me](mailto:bowenyu@mit.edu) if you would like to give it a try!
